# Data and figure licensing

The BSD 3-Clause `LICENSE` applies to original source code only.

## Row-level data

The modelling cohorts combine values extracted or transformed from multiple publications, theses, and database-derived collections. No blanket licence is asserted over those third-party-derived values. A row-level record may be added to `data/public/` only when its source-specific licence, terms, or author ownership has been documented as permitting redistribution.

The current `data/source_index/source_index.csv` records all 260 source groups as `Review_required`. It is a bibliographic and provenance index, not a grant of rights to the underlying records.

## Aggregate results

Files in `results/summary/` and the aggregate files in `figure_data/` were generated by the released analysis. They intentionally exclude the full processed modelling tables and row-level OOF prediction file.

## Figures

The figures were generated by the author from the released aggregate outputs. A separate Creative Commons licence may be added for figures and documentation after confirming compatibility with the journal publishing agreement. Until then, no broader licence is asserted beyond uses permitted by applicable law.
